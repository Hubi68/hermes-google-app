Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This application ("Hermes Agent – Personal Google Workspace Connector") is a private tool used solely by its owner, Hubertus Oswald, to let a personal assistant running on the owner's own computer access the owner's own Google account.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app may access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts data of the owner's account.

Use and storage

Data is used only to carry out tasks requested by the owner. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
