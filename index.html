Hermes Agent – Personal Google Workspace Connector

This is a private, single-user application operated by Hubertus Oswald. It lets a personal AI assistant (Hermes Agent) running on the owner's own computer access the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts.

The app is not offered to the public and is not intended for use by anyone other than its owner.
